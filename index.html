Our Story

This started in my DMs.

Since 2023, I have been helping skilled professionals land remote jobs. What started as answering the same questions over and over again in my DMs, including "How do I get a remote job?", "Why is nobody responding to my applications?" and "What am I doing wrong?", became HireCirca.

I built this because I kept seeing the same pattern. Skilled people. Real qualifications. Real experience. Getting overlooked, not because they were not good enough, but because nobody taught them how the hiring system actually works.

Nobody told them about ATS. Nobody told them about ghost jobs. Nobody taught them how to cold email their way into a conversation before a job was ever posted.

I have watched members go from months of rejection to offer letters in weeks. I have watched people who did not believe remote work was possible for them land roles with companies outside Nigeria. I have watched someone negotiate from $5 an hour to $8 an hour and get it.

This is not a motivational page. This is a strategy community. And it works.
